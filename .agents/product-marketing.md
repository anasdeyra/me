## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** The parts of a product that earn money (booking, checkout, supplier integrations) are slow, fragile or missing a feature competitors have, and the business can't hire a team to fix it.
**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Cheap freelancers: code works on day one, breaks under load or with a new supplier.
- Agencies: expensive, slow, junior staff behind a senior pitch.
- Hiring full-time: slow, and the problem is now.

**What it costs them:** Lost bookings, customers sent to competitors or the airline, outages during peak season.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of overpaying for work they can't judge; fear of being left with code nobody understands.

## Switching Dynamics
