## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Full-stack engineer who ships polished software products for startup founders: state-of-the-art UX, performance and security, end to end.
**What it does:** Anas Deyra designs (or builds from your designs) and ships web products: MVPs, features on existing apps, marketing sites, and ongoing fractional work. Quality is the deliverable: fast, accessible, secure, and finished-feeling. Based in Tunis, Tunisia (UTC+1). He is also a research master's student in Information Systems and publishes his research projects as /work entries (type: research) (credibility for recruiters and engineers; not a client-facing pillar).
**Product category:** Freelance full-stack / product engineer (how buyers search: "freelance full-stack developer", "MVP developer", "hire React/Node developer"). For recruiters: "full-stack engineer".
**Product type:** Service (freelance) + candidate profile (actively job hunting).
**Business model:** Custom quotes per project (no public prices; process explained), retainers for ongoing work. Also available through Upwork (escrow lowers first-time buyer risk).

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** Startups, pre-seed to Series A, worldwide (past clients in Singapore, Germany, France, India, Tunisia, Spain, US and more).
**Decision-makers:** Founders (often non-technical or solo-technical). Secondary: recruiters/HR and hiring engineers for full-time full-stack roles.
**Primary use case:** A founder needs a product built or improved to a professional standard without hiring a team yet.
**Jobs to be done:**

- Turn an idea or spec into a launched, credible MVP.
- Ship features on an existing app without breaking it or adding debt.
- Get a fast, polished marketing site or landing page that converts.
- Have a senior dev on call (retainer / fractional) for maintenance and decisions.

**Use cases:**

- Pre-launch MVP build (with or without designs).
- Post-launch quality pass: performance, accessibility, security hardening.
- Overflow capacity for a small team.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Founders need a product that looks and works like it was built by a strong team, but can only afford one person, and can't evaluate that person's quality upfront.
**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Cheap offshore freelancers: functional but unpolished, rewrites later.
- Agencies: expensive, slow, junior staff behind a senior sales pitch.
- No-code: hits limits, poor performance, lock-in.
- Hiring full-time: slow, expensive, premature pre-PMF.

**What it costs them:** Rewrites, delayed launches, lost user trust, lost investor confidence.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of being ghosted, fear of overpaying for low quality, feeling unable to judge technical work.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Other freelance full-stack devs (Upwork, Malt, Toptal), which fall short on polish/UX or communication.
**Secondary:** Small agencies and dev shops, which cost more and move slower, with less senior attention.
**Indirect:** No-code tools, technical co-founder search, hiring in-house.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Polish as the deliverable: UX, performance, accessibility and security from the start rather than added at the end.
- Verified record: Upwork Top Rated, 100% Job Success, 5.0 rating, real client reviews.
- International: clients in 7 countries (Singapore, Germany, France, India, Tunisia, Spain, US); works in English, French, Arabic; based in Tunis (UTC+1, no DST), so working hours overlap fully with Europe.
- Design flexibility: bring your designs or get them made.
- Research-grade rigor (research master's in Information Systems) behind engineering decisions.

**How we do it differently:** Quality budgets (performance, a11y, security) are agreed upfront and measured; regular async updates.
**Why that's better:** Less rework, faster load times, a product users trust from day one.
**Why customers choose us:** The proof (Upwork record + case study metrics) plus the portfolio itself as a demonstration (Spec mode).
