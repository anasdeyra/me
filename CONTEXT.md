# Context

Glossary for the portfolio's copy and content. Terms only; decisions live in AGENTS.md.

## Positioning

**Product engineer**: the positioning label for Anas: an engineer who owns the product outcome (what the business gains), not only the code. Used in client-facing copy, meta and the profile title.
_Avoid_: freelancer, developer for hire, "solutions".

**Job title**: the title actually held in a role (Metis: Software Engineer). Shown as-is in CV experience entries; never replaced by the positioning label.
